# Heart Disease Analysis Dashboard — Excel

![Dashboard Preview](Dashboard%20Preview.png)
## Project Overview

This project analyses the UCI Heart Disease dataset to identify patterns associated with heart disease across demographic and clinical factors.

The analysis was conducted in Microsoft Excel using Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPIs, and interactive slicers to develop a healthcare analytics dashboard.

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to explore:

- How is heart disease distributed across different age groups?
- How does heart disease prevalence differ by sex?
- Which chest pain types are more commonly associated with heart disease?
- Is exercise-induced angina associated with heart disease?
- How does thalassemia status vary between patients with and without heart disease?
- How does average cholesterol vary across age groups and disease status?
- How does average maximum heart rate differ between patients with and without heart disease?

## Dataset

- **Source:** UCI Heart Disease Dataset
- **Records:** 920 patients
- **Variables:** 17
- **Domain:** Cardiovascular / Clinical Healthcare Data

The dataset contains demographic and clinical variables including age, sex, chest pain type, resting blood pressure, cholesterol, maximum heart rate, exercise-induced angina, ST depression, slope, number of major vessels, thalassemia, and heart disease status.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared in Excel using Power Query before analysis.

The preparation process included reviewing data types, reviewing missing values, standardising fields where required, and creating analysis-ready variables age groups and heart disease status.

The prepared dataset was then used for exploratory analysis through PivotTables and PivotCharts.

## Exploratory Data Analysis

PivotTables and PivotCharts were used to explore relationships between heart disease status and key demographic and clinical variables.

The analysis included:

- Age group and heart disease status
- Sex and heart disease status
- Chest pain type and heart disease status
- Exercise-induced angina and heart disease status
- Thalassemia status and heart disease status
- Average cholesterol by age group and disease status
- Average maximum heart rate by disease status

## Dashboard

The final dashboard brings together key patient metrics, demographic and clinical analysis, and interactive slicers in a single view.

Users can explore the analysis using:

- Age Group
- Sex
- Chest Pain Type
- Heart Disease Status

The dashboard includes KPI cards and visualisations designed to provide a concise overview of the dataset.

## Key Insights

- Heart disease was present in **509 of 920 patients**, representing **55.33%** of the dataset.
- The distribution of heart disease varied across different age groups.
- Chest pain type showed different distributions between patients with and without heart disease.
- Exercise-induced angina showed different distributions by heart disease status.
- Thalassemia status showed differences between patients with and without heart disease.
- Average cholesterol and maximum heart rate varied across disease status and age groups.

These observations describe patterns within this dataset and should not be interpreted as clinical or causal conclusions.
